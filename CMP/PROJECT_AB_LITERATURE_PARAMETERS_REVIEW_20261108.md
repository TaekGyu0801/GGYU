## 1. Direct primary and peer reviewed sources / scientific scope
1. **Wu et al. 2023**, *Physical mechanisms on the size-effect in GaN-based Micro-LEDs*, Micro and Nanostructures 177, 207542; DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micrna.2023.207542; primary PDF user uploaded. Sec.1.1 10µm, nGaN3.9µm Si5e18, four In0.08 QW3nm GaN barriers8nm, Mg input2e19, 5nm bilateral acceptor-like sidewall trap. Sec.2.2 Fig4-5 supports sidewall acceptor charge, lateral electric field/current and QW edge radiative drop *in author's Silvaco model*, not yet measured in school's Sentaurus at low matched-J. Fig1(b) schematically AlGaN but thickness unknown.
2. **Cao et al. 2023**, Scientific Reports 13, 14820 (with publisher-reported Apr2024 Fig2 correction), DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41678-1. 2D Sentaurus high-voltage **GaN-on-Si HEMT/buffer**, not an LED. Their *bulk total C concentration* `1e19 cm^-3` in *2µm GaN:C layer* is DIFFERENT from `C_N` acceptor trap concentrations: `6e16,4e17,6e17,6e18 cm^-3`. `C_Ga=0.5*C_N` donor trap; `C_N E=Ev+0.9eV`, `C_Ga E=Ec-0.11eV`, `sigma_e=sigma_h=1e-15cm2` for both. Their Poole-Frenkel, SRH, thermionic, trap-assisted tunneling physics are specific to that transport problem; not blindly import all to LED or guarantee optical improvement. `C_N=1e19` or `2e19` **NOT reported** in their density sweep; do NOT present as proven.
3. **Richter et al. 2020**, Crystal Research and Technology 55, 1900129, DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/crat.201900129. Experimental intentionally C-doped bulk GaN maximal specific resistivity ~`2e10 Ωcm` at total C `8.8e18cm^-3`; higher C (to `3.5e19`) may increase conductivity via self-compensation/passivation. Activation `~0.94eV`. Thick bulk HVPE material vs LED 200nm region — DO NOT assign this measured resistivity to thin in-device region without actual model/verification.
4. **C-related defect physics review**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/pssa.202100235: `C_N` acceptor often `Ev+0.9..1.1eV`. Supports consistency of Cao `Ev+0.9`, but other carbon-related traps exist; not guarantee one defect is adequate.
5. **Furtmayr et al. 2011**, PRB 84, 205303, DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.84.205303; GaN/AlGaN nanowire and lateral shell with xAl `0.08..1`; lateral electric fields and emission can worsen at high Al, so high Al is not monotonically better. **NOT** etched 10µm planar-Mesa QW-edge replacement.
6. **Wu, Y. et al. 2022**, Light: Science & Applications 11, 294, DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41377-022-00985-4; actual selective-area grown InGaN/AlGaN nanowire µLED with GaN/AlGaN shell and AlGaN barriers ~`xAl=0.15`; QWs *6×12nm In0.3*, AlGaN barriers15nm and polarization-induced carrier redistribution. Supports **AlGaN composition feasibility** and importance of lateral shell/polarization, NOT a 20nm planar guard width or guaranteed sidewall-loss reduction in this Wu10µm mesa.
7. **Baek et al. 2023**, Nature Communications 14, 1386 DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-36773-w; 10×10µm InGaN microLED; **vertical** GaN quantum barriers 3.5/7.5/10.5nm, redesigned **vertical AlInGaN EBL** 17nm Al0.20In0.02Ga0.78N Mg2e18 →45nm Al0.12In0.02Ga0.86N Mg2e19. Improved balanced low-current carrier injection, radiative efficiency and lower surface paths. Neither 17 nor45nm is a literature-supported planar **lateral AlGaN guard width**; preserve current baseline's EBL0 for isolated A/B comparisons.
8. **Gong et al. 2026**, Photonics 13, 806 DOI https://doi.org/10.3390/photonics13090806; actual 5–50µm InGaN **QW–QD composite** with **1nm vertical Al0.1Ga0.9N interlayer**, 20nm **vertical p-AlGaN EBL**. No quantified pure lateral guard improvement. May bound exploratory xAl0.10 but *cannot* be used as evidence for 1nm lateral shell.
9. **Chang et al. 2020**, Nanoscale Res Lett 15:160 DOI https://doi.org/10.1186/s11671-020-03372-3 (open PMC https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7410900/): thinning *vertical* QBs can reduce current spreading in some designs, while Baek found thicker QBs better in different structures. Avoid assuming one universally optimal vertical barrier solution.
