## Replacement Project A recommendation: selective pGaN Mg deactivation / current confinement
- Mechanism: Mg-H complex / hydrogen plasma treatment passivates p-GaN edge acceptors => locally high resistance => fewer holes arrive at damaged mesa sidewall. Anchor Kirilenko et al., InGaN-based green micro-LED efficiency enhancement by hydrogen passivation of the p-GaN sidewall, Applied Physics Express 15 084003 (2022), DOI 10.35848/1882-0786/ac7fdc. Studied 20 and 100 um green LEDs; reported 20um EQE x1.4 and reverse leakage >10x lower. This is NOT a 10um quantitative prediction.
- Other anchor Hang et al., Artificially formed resistive ITO/p-GaN junction to suppress the current spreading and decrease the surface recombination for GaN-based micro-light emitting diodes, Opt Express 29 31201–31211 (2021), PMID34615218. Resistive contacts are distinct from Mg deactivation.
- Starting pilot only: pGaN x0..0.120um, edge y0..0.200um and y9.800..10um including 5nm Dmg edge for full-width anode not to bypass. Central pGaN nominal Mg2e19, edge effective Mg2e16cm-3, entirely H/acceptor availability **surrogate**. Keep Dmg traps unchanged, full contact unchanged, verify in PP. y width, Mg magnitude are purely CMP uncalibrated assumptions, NOT hydrogen plasma dose, reported experiment values or validated conductivity. One 5V run. Prior CMP/PROJECT_F_FRING_DESIGN_20261119.md is similar electrical architecture but F inspired; literature framing now H Mg deactivation if adopted, keep separate provenance.
- Main risks: changing Mg changes space charge, not merely resistance; full-width ideal Ohmic boundary may bypass local effect; high voltage penalty; baseline proprietary source inaccessible; verify local hDensity and hCurrent as validation gate.

## Replacement Project B recommendation: quantum barrier thinning to control vertical vs lateral current spreading
- Anchor Le Chang et al., Alternative Strategy to Reduce Surface Recombination for InGaN/GaN Micro-light-Emitting Diodes—Thinning the Quantum Barriers to Manage the Current Spreading, Nanoscale Research Letters 15, 160 (2020), DOI 10.1186/s11671-020-03372-3. Paper used 10x10um blue InGaN devices, QB=6,9,12nm, compared with/without sidewall defects; 6nm promoted hole injection and reduced lateral spreading into defects. They use APSYS with Schrödinger+Poisson+drift-diffusion, different EBL, In content, 0.5um defective width, trap levels. Their measured/calc EQE should NOT be ported to Wu model.
- Proposed CMP one-case pilot: baseline all five GaN QB thicknesses tQB=8nm -> 6nm while retaining four 3nm In0.08GaN QWs, all original sidewall damage traps and region associations, same p/n doping/contact/physics/solver; width10um; regenerate SDE/mesh, especially x well positions; no added materials, no AlGaN. Changed MQW total span from 52 to 42nm (40+12 vs 30+12), nGaN start shifts from x0.172 to0.162um if pGaN0.120 and EBL0. No assurance of quantum transport/tunneling behavior because current school Sentaurus deck may lack Schrödinger or nonlocal tunneling. First check model adequacy and compare current density, QW carrier distribution, absolute radiative rate at same current. 6nm is literature tested but not proven optimal for Wu In0.08/5nm damage.
- Major confounds: total active-stack height and contacts shift, thinner barriers can increase electron leakage / change electron-hole balance; no external optical LEE in current deck, no EQE conclusions; some newer 2026 green LEDs favor thicker QB for different metrics, so not universal. Detailed material/quantum model needed before quantitative claim.
