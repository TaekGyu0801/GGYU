## User's concern
User asked if Project A locally removes Si/adds carbon traps whereas Project B replaces portions of original MQWs and QB with AlGaN, whether it is scientifically logical to compare, and whether improvements must be addition-only to the baseline.

## Answer with explicit evidence classes
- **DESIGN/INFERENCE:** Additive-only is **NOT a general scientific requirement**. Valid device engineering may change doping, heterostructures, dimensions, electrodes, regrowth or surface states. Project A is not strictly addition-only either: its *local Si donor is set 5e18→0*, plus C_N and C_Ga defects, while GaN host and MQWs are retained.
- **RECOVERED SCHOOL WU 3.2 SDE:** baseline pGaN/MQW/nGaN, 10µm lateral Y, 4×3nm InGaN wells and 5×8nm GaN barriers, bilateral 5nm Dmg. B1 initial frozen design x=.120–.172um, yL=.005-.025, yR=9.975-9.995um replaces 20nm per side of 9 active-stack layers. Damaged 5nm remains untouched, but **40nm of 10µm (0.4%) projected original InGaN QW width becomes AlGaN**. 0.4% of width does NOT imply 0.4% emission because edge recombination may be highly nonuniform.
- **CONFUNDING:** Baseline vs B1 mixes (i) lost InGaN emitter area and modified QW/carrier density, (ii) AlGaN band offset/polarization/transport, (iii) extra heterointerfaces and possible altered geometry/mesh. Bnull (same exact 20nm material replacement using GaN instead of AlGaN) partly controls equal QW-volume loss: compare B1-Bnull alloy-material effect under matched carrier injection, Bnull-baseline area-removal effect, and B1-baseline net engineering benefit. Need calculate absolute QW Radiative for remaining InGaN, original emitter volumetric efficiency, one net trap pair-loss, same physical terminal I, V penalty and maps. Bnull is **not a perfect placebo** because GaN itself introduces different band offset/strain/polarization compared with InGaN; it is a controlled alternative host only. Mesh and model parity essential.
- **ALTERNATIVES:**
  1. External AlGaN sidewall shell: y outside original Mesa (e.g. if 20nm shell proposed, y[-.020,0] and[10,10.020]), x stack .120–.172. Keeps baseline InGaN/Dmg widths and full 10µm conductive core but **changes outer footprint/interfaces/contact geometry**, requires material strain/passivation interface models and may NOT create a lateral barrier between center QW and internal 5nm Dmg traps (shell is *outside* those traps). Therefore cannot claim equivalent sidewall carrier confinement or unchanged total device geometry. The 20nm physical thickness external is only a proposed modeling value, not a published optimum; **NOT chosen**.
  2. Convert only 20nm near-edge parts of original five **GaN QB** layers to AlGaN while keeping 4 InGaN QWs fully original. Preserves QW volume but vertical AlGaN strips disconnected at QW locations: not a continuous lateral barrier, will affect vertical injection and band maps; mechanism differs, and still materials replaced in QBs.
  3. Exterior dielectric passivation for surface states, e.g. ALD Al2O3 or SiO2 and physically justified trap-interface models: literature-supported surface treatment mechanism but **different intervention hypothesis** from lateral AlGaN carrier blocking; original internal 5nm trap effect may persist.
  4. Maintain old B1 and Bnull (internal substitution) as initial **physics screening**; state explicitly future manufacturability/regrowth and processing compatibility are not demonstrated.
- **PUBLIC LITERATURE:** *Advanced technologies in InGaN micro-LED fabrication to mitigate the sidewall effect* (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41377-025-01751-y, categorizes sidewall defect removal, surface passivation, carrier transport pathway control as different approaches; surface passivation affects external states more than deeper penetration damage. 2022 WuY nanowire AlGaN shell context is NOT wafer etched 10µm lateral replacement proof.
- **GEOMETRY IMPOSSIBILITY:** With fixed full lateral width10µm, fixed outer internal5nm damage strips and all remaining original InGaN QW edge volume fixed, it is impossible to add a *distinct, finite-width interior AlGaN layer between intact emitter and original damaged edge* without either replacing an existing material region, shifting boundaries/expanding device, or changing overall stack. A true separating guard requires geometric space.
