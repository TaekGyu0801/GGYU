## 2026-11-03 — Why 5nm sidewall damaged region? Geometry vs electrically affected area (SOURCE CHECK + DERIVATION, NO RUN)
**Worker:** 주수빈. **User challenge:** CMP hypothesizes smaller μLEDs have greater relative damage impact, yet 5nm Dmg is far smaller geometrically than Kou's 4μm dead-zone. Verify whether 5nm is logically defensible.

**DIRECT Wu original publisher source:** Wu et al., `Physical mechanisms on the size-effect in GaN-based Micro-LEDs`, *Micro and Nanostructures* 177 (2023) 207542, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micrna.2023.207542. Article's device-method description explicitly: a 10μm example with four In0.08GaN 3nm wells, GaN 8nm barriers and **acceptor-like traps within 5nm of both sidewall edges**; authors attribute size effects to sidewall-trap-driven depletion and lateral-current diversion. **5nm is Wu's TCAD implementation width**, NOT a universal experimental measurement of physical etch damage or the total optically/electrically affected zone. CMP took the lateral trapping idea, not Wu's vertical epitaxy (Kou's 4QW/In0.15/22nm barrier differs). CMP nominal Nt=1e18, EtEv+0.75 and σ1e-15 are calibration starting values, not automatically Wu's exact calibrated trap set.

**Independent published evidence that geometric damage ≠ effective emission dead-zone:** Liu et al., *Light: Science & Applications* 2025 https://doi.org/10.1038/s41377-025-01751-y reviews physical etch damage depths from ~2nm up to dozens of nm, with observed performance-affected edge reach several hundred nm to microns depending on material/process; note different measurements/definitions. Wang et al., *Optics Letters* 48 (2023) 4845–4848, DOI https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.501546, reports fluorescence-lifetime-imaging affected fraction ~23% even in 80µm mesas and nearly all of a 10µm mesa under their fabrication conditions; not directly transferable to CMP 4µm. Physical trap charge can alter bands/currents/carrier diffusion far beyond 5nm. Wu's premise is compatible with this.

**Geometric-only derivation at fixed d=5nm=0.005µm:** ideal square L×L four-side damaged fraction `f=1-((L-2d)/L)^2`; L=4µm ->0.499375%, 10->0.1999%, 20->0.099975%, 60->~0.0333306%, 100->0.019999%. Thus 20→4µm approximately 5× increase in *fraction* even at constant 5nm, satisfying directional size-effect hypothesis, but the magnitude alone is NOT computed η_IQE, trap SRH or effective dead-zone. For actual CMP 2D planar full/half stripes, two-side **width** ratio is `2d/L`, 4µm ->0.25%, not the 0.499% physical square's area ratio; comparison needs proper sidewall perimeter/area calibration (P/A square=4/L, 2D strip=2/L). Previous Kou ~64% in 20µm sample was for a model-specific 4µm EACH edge dead-zone and is **not a like-for-like structural damage thickness**.

**Trap sheet-density equivalence, conditional:** model uniform volumetric `Nt=1e18cm^-3` over `d=5e-7cm` gives `Ns≈Nt*d=5e11cm^-2` PER EDGE as an integrated density, but this is NOT automatically the effective surface recombination velocity; trap energy, cross-section, occupancy, local carriers and band bending matter. Changing d at fixed Nt also changes Ns, so attributing observed change to geometric width alone is invalid. An alternative width sensitivity at fixed Ns would scale Nt inversely, subject to nonlinear electrostatics and structural quality.

**Assessment:** 5nm is a documented, *logically plausible thin trap-placement region* used in previous TCAD research. It is NOT physically calibrated for CMP's 4µm etched microLED, and no completed 4/10/20µm size-dependent sweep exists in documented status. The research hypothesis is about rising perimeter/area and **effective carrier access to sidewall defects**, not necessarily a large *fraction of physical 5nm damaged semiconductor*. Never inflate Wdmg just to make the geometric ratio resemble Kou; equally, never assert 5nm is sufficient without sensitivity tests.

**NEXT PROPOSAL (NO APPROVED RUN):** (1) preserve 5nm Wu-inspired reference as control until user/team approves a common parameter revision; (2) first demonstrate at identical geometry/current that Nt OFF→ON produces localized gap-state trap loss, edge n/p depletion and lower current-normalized radiative yield; (3) test 4,10,20µm size sweep at same Wdmg/Nt and same physical/current normalization; (4) separate explicit Wdmg=5/20/50nm *illustrative sensitivity* at fixed Nt (physical damage depth) from controlled fixed Ns numerical-width study, recording source/provenance; (5) compare 2D stripe versus actual square 4-wall geometry limitations. A/B must retain identical damage parameters when doing fair comparison. Need 5nm sidewall mesh verification and convergence. No source/deck or simulation changed.
